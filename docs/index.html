About

Welcome to my homepage! I am an undergraduate student majoring in Electrical Engineering at Zhejiang University. I expect to continue my academic journey at The University of Hong Kong in Fall 2027 as a PhD student in Electrical Engineering.

My current research focuses on wind power generation and the control of electric motors and drives. For my doctoral research, I am particularly interested in exploring edge intelligence for smart grids.
